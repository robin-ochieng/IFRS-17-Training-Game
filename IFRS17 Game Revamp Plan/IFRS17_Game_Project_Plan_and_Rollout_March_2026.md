The goal is to run repeatable, sponsor-led rollouts per insurer and close March with clear adoption evidence, engagement metrics, and a ready-to-scale playbook.Scope and Success MetricsClient adoption: 10 insurers launched with a named sponsor, company code (or invite link) enabled, and at least one staff cohort onboarded.Engagement: each launched insurer achieves a defined participation baseline (e.g., 30-200 users depending on size) and measurable activity (weekly active players, completion rates).Product: admin question bank + segmentation, tournament-only questions workflow (AI draft + Leslie verification), company leaderboards, email and certificate automation.Reporting: weekly snapshot to leadership covering participation, completion, average score, and top performers per company.Target InsurersTop 10 (as primary March targets):Kenya Reinsurance Corporation HQJubilee Health Insurance LimitedIntra Africa Insurance Company LimitedMonarch Life Insurance Company LimitedPioneer Insurance Company LimitedBUPA Global Insurance LimitedOld Mutual General Insurance Kenya LimitedAAR Insurance KenyaGeminia Insurance Company Limited - LifeMonarch General Insurance Company LimitedAdditional insurers (pipeline / optional if capacity allows):Kenya Orient Insurance CompanyFidelity Shield Insurance Company LimitedThe Heritage Insurance Company LimitedAlliance Insurance LimitedCurrent Game SnapshotThe current IFRS 17 Game user experience includes a module-based learning path, a live leaderboard, player scoring (score, streak, level, combo), a timed question flow, and sign-in/sign-up gating for locked modules. This existing structure is a strong base for a company rollout model (company codes, company-only boards) and differentiated question sets by audience.Product Enhancements - Requirements and Acceptance Criteria1. Admin Question Bank (Q&A Database)Key requirements:Admin can create, edit, archive questions and answers.Each question is tagged by: (a) level (Beginner/Standard/Expert), (b) module/topic, and (c) status (Draft/Approved/Archived).Bulk import/export (CSV/Excel) to accelerate population.Acceptance criteria:Admin portal lists questions with filters (level, module, status) and search.Only Approved questions appear in gameplay.2. Tournament-only Questions (Expert)Key requirements:AI-assisted drafting of tournament questions into Draft status.Verification workflow where Leslie (or designated SME) approves questions/answers.Acceptance criteria:Tournament mode pulls only Approved Expert questions.Audit trail: who approved and when.3. Company-level Ranking (Company Leaderboards)Key requirements:Each player is associated with a company (via company code, invite link, or admin assignment).Company-only leaderboard view, plus optional global leaderboard with filters.Support for non-technical question set for broader staff competitions.Acceptance criteria:Company leaderboard shows rank, score, and basic stats for that company only.Admin can create/manage companies and generate company codes/links.4. Email and Certificates IntegrationKey requirements:Automated email notifications (welcome, reminders, completion).Certificate issuance triggered by completion and/or score threshold.Optional weekly sponsor summary email (participation and top performers).Acceptance criteria:Certificate can be generated and delivered reliably to player email.Basic email templates are configurable (branding, signatures).Question Bank Assessment (Sample File Review)The provided sample question file contains 217 questions across multiple IFRS 17 modules.
